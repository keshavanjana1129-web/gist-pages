GIST Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Introduction

Welcome to GIST. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Our Promise: Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Email address (for account creation)
  • Password (encrypted)

Notification Data:

  • App names that send notifications
  • Notification content (for AI summarization)
  • Timestamps

How We Use Your Information

  • AI Summarization: We use Google Gemini AI to generate summaries.
  • Account Management: To maintain your account and process subscriptions.
  • App Improvement: To improve our service.

Data Storage and Retention

You control how long notifications are stored (8 hours to unlimited). When you delete your account, all data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini AI: For notification summarization
  • Razorpay: For payment processing
  • MongoDB: For secure data storage

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Deletion: Delete your account and data
  • Opt-out: Disable specific app notifications

Children's Privacy

GIST is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
