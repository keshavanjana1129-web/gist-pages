Frequently Asked Questions

How does GIST summarize my notifications?

GIST uses Google Gemini AI to read your notifications and create concise summaries in real-time.

Is my data safe and private?

Yes! Your privacy is our priority. We never sell your personal information. You control which apps GIST can access.

How do swipe gestures work?

Swipe left to delete. Swipe right to snooze (choose 1, 2, 3, 4, or 9 hours).

What happens after my free trial?

After your 7-day free trial, subscribe to continue. We offer monthly, quarterly, and annual plans.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Manage your subscription in your device's app store settings (Settings > Subscriptions on iOS).

Can I choose which apps GIST reads?

Yes! Go to Settings in the app to toggle each app on or off.
