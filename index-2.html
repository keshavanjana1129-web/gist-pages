FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
↓
Yes — cancel from App Settings at any time. No questions asked, no hidden fees, no automatic renewals. Your data stays on your device.
Do I need to create an account?
↓
Yes, a simple sign-in is required. This helps sync your preferences and subscription — but all notification data stays 100% local on your device and is never uploaded.
Is my notification data actually private?
↓
Absolutely. All notification data is processed and stored entirely on your device. It never leaves your phone — not for AI, not for analytics. Zero servers involved.
Which apps does Gist work with?
↓
Gist works with all 208 apps on your phone — WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack, Instagram, banking apps, Swiggy, Zomato, LinkedIn. You control which ones Gist reads.
How long can I keep notifications?
↓
As long as you want. No auto-deletion. Store a day, a month, or a year's worth of notifications — your device, your choice.
How do I win the Sudoku discount?
↓
Win 5 Sudoku games per day for 15 consecutive days to earn a 2% subscription discount voucher. Easy, Medium, and Hard all count.
